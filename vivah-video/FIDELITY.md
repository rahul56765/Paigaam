# Vivah fidelity notes

Reference: 57.94s vertical screen recording (480×852 at 30fps). The first displayed frame is a player/screen-recording artifact; it is not part of the authored film.

## Preserved from the reference
- The Ganesha opening and the invitation's ordered progression through family/couple details, date/event cards, illustrated ceremony moments and the QR ending.
- A distinct, fixed-position logo scene immediately after the Ganesha introduction. A transparent customer PNG and the initials fallback occupy the same 470×470 px frame and use the same reveal.
- A soft floral-arch visual language and two customer-selectable palettes, with original Paigaam art.
- Original fixed story clips, generated once for the template; there is no customer-time AI generation.
- No bundled soundtrack. MP3 and MP4-with-audio uploads are used as customer-provided background audio.

## Differences and limits
1. All illustrations are original; no reference artwork, logo, names or addresses are reused. The sample is Meera and Arjun, the Sharma and Verma families, and a fictional Jaipur venue.
2. The recording is low-resolution and contains player UI, so exact cut points and microscopic easing cannot be read reliably from this upload. The implementation therefore follows the observed approximately 58-second flow and transition character, not a claim of frame-identical timing.
3. The newly illustrated couple and Jaipur arch are original interpretations, not copies of the reference characters or architectural motifs.
4. Story media is authored at 900×1600 and the Remotion composition renders the complete film at 1080×1920/30fps; the render is full HD, but the source clips are upscaled to the output canvas.
5. The optional logo file must be PNG with transparency. The optional QR image is PNG. Upload geometry and animation do not move when either is present.
6. MP4 background uploads must contain an audio stream; only the first audio stream is used, and the video track is discarded.
7. The 16:9 format centres the unchanged portrait composition; its internal layout is not redesigned.
