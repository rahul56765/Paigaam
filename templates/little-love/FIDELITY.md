# Fidelity and known limits

## Preserved

- All five pages, their sequence, chocolate-brown/ivory palette, landscape desktop sheet, dotted surrounding canvas, restrained small typography and large negative spaces.
- Bouquet cover, photo/letter collage, irregular lipstick-paper corner, four-picture strip, penguins, hearts, two different audio-player shapes, cat cutouts and closing doodles.
- Two transition families: a 70% shrink/hold/Y-flip/grow at the outer boundaries, and a clipped horizontal slide for interior pages. Reference review is in REFERENCE.md.
- Thin outside chevrons, opening CTA, music record and green playlist popover, waveform progress/seek, song progress and transport.

## Not a pixel-exact asset clone

- Photographs and sticker assets from the reference site are not shipped. Bouquets, penguins and cats are newly generated original assets. Song art is an original anonymous close-up of dark jackets and white wired earphones. Sample couple photographs come from PAIGAAM's existing demo library and are replaced by customer uploads.
- Lipstick paper, hearts and ending doodles are original SVG approximations. Lip marks are vector-patterned rather than photographic ink impressions. The sticker contours, precise flower shapes, cat faces and doodle strokes therefore differ.
- Pixelify Sans is the closest selected locally hosted pixel-like display/body font; the original font is unknown. Homemade Apple approximates the two handwritten stickers. Decorative ornaments are reconstructed, not exact transcriptions.
- Colors are matched to a filmed monitor; they cannot be recovered as exact original CSS values.
- Flips take 1.4 seconds, including shrink/hold/turn/grow; interior slides take 350 ms. Hold time between pages is controlled by the reader, never forced to the filming duration.
- No copyrighted reference audio or reference-site branding is bundled. The default 24-second instrumental is newly synthesized for this template; the memories default is an instrumental demonstration, not a fabricated voice recording. Customers can upload their own memory recording and choose their own song/playlist URLs.
- Native audio reproduces the compact players. A supplied YouTube song also opens a visible YouTube player after the user's tap; provider restrictions and video availability can prevent playback. This is disclosed in the UI. No claim is made that YouTube playback has been verified without a real available video.
- The cover hearts use CSS motion and differ from the source particles. The record text uses PAIGAAM-neutral wording instead of an unreadable source playlist name.

## Responsive and accessibility choices

The video only establishes a landscape desktop composition. Desktop and landscape layouts preserve it. On portrait phones, the same five pages use a taller sheet with the same content/art and adjusted placement so text is not reduced to illegible desktop proportions. This is an explicit adaptation, not evidence of the source's mobile behavior. Long custom letters fit their reserved area; maximum-length copy can scroll within it rather than being truncated. Swipe, keyboard navigation, page dots, page counter, replay, reduced-motion support and accessible media labels are additional implementation affordances. With scripts disabled, every card remains readable in a scroll stack; interactivity is unavailable.

## Commercial launch checks

This is a love-card template, not a wedding/event invitation: the reference contains no date, venue, RSVP or countdown. No wedding sections have been invented. The configured initial price is zero for new listings, consistent with the neighboring love-card family; existing owner-managed prices are untouched. Review pricing in Admin before commercial launch. No production invitation, payment, existing template or customer data is modified by this change.
