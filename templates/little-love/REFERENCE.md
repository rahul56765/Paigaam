## Proven versus inferred

Thin side chevrons, opening CTA, waveform seek highlighting, record/music control and playlist overlay are visible. Continuous audio playback, exact tracks/durations, keyboard controls, swipes, mobile composition, replay, and previous/next track behavior are not proven. Functional keyboard/swipe navigation, a replay action, actual seekable media and selectable playlist tracks are implementation additions consistent with those controls, not claims about unseen source behavior. The exact playlist title is clipped and is not invented as a supposed transcription.

The reference is low-bitrate footage of a monitor, with compression, perspective, moiré and changing exposure. Exact source CSS, fonts, ornaments, track durations and photographic source files cannot be recovered from it. See FIDELITY.md for deliberate substitutions and responsive choices.
