# Inaam — Gift For You!

A gift-box landing for Boyfriend Day: three illustrated cat gifts on a cream
card, and whichever he taps, the lids pop and the whole site unwraps — love
coupons he stamps REDEEMED, a Best Boyfriend Award under a swaying gold
rosette, a denim pocket with photos tucked in beside a letter on star-paper,
and a finale polaroid with your song (YouTube embeds; Spotify/Apple get a
play pill). A cheeky "no" link (or three taps that miss the gifts) flashes a
crying-cat "TRY AGAIN!" interstitial and returns on its own.

- Slug: `inaam` — `/create/inaam`, `/inaam/demo`, `/p/inaam-<hex>`
- Family: bfday (shared engine: wizard, draft/upload/publish, previews)
- Assets: `public/assets/inaam/{gift-1,gift-2,gift-3,crying-cat,rosette}.png`
  and `public/inaam/og.jpg` are healed at boot from `lib/bfday/assets.js`
- Free (`price: 0`), no account, instant publish
