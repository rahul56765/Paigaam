# Sita Kalyanam

A reusable Hindu wedding invitation within PAIGAAM's existing bfday family engine. No separate application, service, database or customer route system is introduced. The reference is a 32.37-second video filmed off a phone. This implementation recreates its continuous vertical flow, devotional hands opener, ivory/peach floral-gold framing, Ram–Sita figures, banana leaves, thin event cards, portrait carousel, three-plus-one countdown tiles and red footer. It is not a claim of pixel-identical source-art recovery: original source layers, exact typeface and transition timings were not supplied. Artwork is newly generated, reference-guided illustration, not extracted original artwork. All typography, dates, names, buttons, counters and customer content are live HTML.

## Order and interactions

Opening → main cover → family blessings (bride then groom) → muhurtham → events → reception → venue → Our Moments → countdown and closing. The nine sections scroll naturally after a tap; they are not a next/previous slide deck. Opening uses one continuous, stationary devotional painting with a softly pulsing fingertip halo; the image itself is never split or moved. Reduced-motion preference skips the delay. No-JavaScript access remains a continuous document with an anchor into the cover. Music never blocks opening.

Gallery: horizontal portrait scroll-snap, swipe, arrows, dots and keyboard; click a photograph for a modal viewer with previous/next, Escape and restored focus. Empty galleries keep the section without fake customer photographs. Upload failures display a clear message and disable the broken photo rather than opening an empty viewer. All images have dimension-preserving containers; illustrations remain contain-fit and customer portraits use cover-fit. Natural scrolling keeps longer customer copy readable rather than clipping it into fixed slides. Optional family and venue photos are supported.

## Customer data

Config, schema, renderer, public interaction/style files and static art are separated. Fictional sample identities, dates and venue exist only in config.demo. The demo's three generated photographs depict fictional people and an illustrative temple setting; no real venue or customer identity is implied. They are not defaults in a customer's blank draft. Static temple art is explicitly illustrative, not a photograph of the fictional venue. Customers can replace it with their actual venue photograph.

Seven existing wizard steps expose names, parents, headings/copy, messages, wedding date/time, UTC offset, reception details, events, addresses, Maps/QR destinations, photographs, music and countdown overrides. Weekday is derived from the selected civil date. Local HH:mm times use the explicitly selected UTC offset (India +05:30 by default). For dates in daylight-saving regions, choose the offset applicable on the wedding date; this template does not infer IANA daylight-saving transitions.

Events support 0–20 cards, add/edit/remove/reorder and repeated kinds. Each event has a schedule source: custom, main wedding, or reception. Linked schedules always follow current date/time fields. A custom schedule requires its own date and time. Empty event venue/address follow the current main venue (or the reception overrides for reception-linked cards). Entered overrides remain independent. Reception venue/address also inherit the main venue when blank. Countdown defaults to the current wedding date and time; optional date/time overrides independently replace those parts. Expired countdowns clamp to zero and show the editable celebration message. Incomplete drafts show dashes, never NaN or a stale sample date.

Gallery supports 0–12 images with captions and reordering. The existing family engine enforces creator ownership, image signatures, upload limits, private drafts and publication. Optional QR is generated by the existing vendored QR encoder, and is omitted when its validated HTTPS destination is blank. Maps uses the customer's validated HTTPS link, otherwise an encoded Google Maps query of the current venue and address. URLs and all customer output are validated/escaped.

Music uses the existing family audio upload contract (MP3, M4A, OGG or WAV, 15 MiB), or validated HTTPS direct media/YouTube. Upload takes precedence over link. Playback is attempted only in a guest gesture and provides play/pause/retry controls. Provider embedding restrictions or browser audio policies may require another tap; no external track is bundled or downloaded. Use music you have rights to share.

## Integration and routes

- templates/sita-kalyanam/{config,schema,render}.js
- public/sita-kalyanam/{invitation.css,invitation.js}
- assets/sita-kalyanam: SHA-256 manifest and base64 artwork
- lib/bfday/assets-sita-kalyanam.js restores checked artwork at existing family startup, including the demo photos.
- templates/bfday.js registers the isolated template; the central registry picks it up automatically.
- /sita-kalyanam/demo, /create/sita-kalyanam, /sita-kalyanam/preview-frame, /api/sita-kalyanam/draft, upload, upload-audio and publish are mounted by existing shared routes. Published invitations retain the standard /p/ URL model.

Only a slug-scoped list-editor branch permits zero rows and clean draft list replacement for this template; existing template list behaviour is unchanged. Its editor gets a distinct script cache key. Paid checkout extends existing protected-template safeguards to Sita Kalyanam only: validated owner-only checkout, locked pending edits, ephemeral storage guard and independent order settlement. Existing creator, deployment and wedding-video behaviour is unchanged. New installs seed this template at ₹99; the existing admin-set database price remains authoritative after seeding. The catalogue thumbnail is 1000 × 688 px and combines the template's opening and cover artwork.
